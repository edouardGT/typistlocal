Privacy

Everything happens on your Mac. What you type is not sent anywhere. There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry.

Updated 18 November 2026

In short

  • The language model runs inside the app, on your Mac.
  • Nothing you type, write or keep in your notes leaves your Mac.
  • Password fields, address bars, search fields and login dialogs are never read.
  • Apps you turn off are not read at all.
  • Suggestions are left out of shared screens, recordings and screenshots.
  • What it keeps of your writing is encrypted, and one menu item deletes it.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting of its own.

What it reads

The app needs the macOS Accessibility permission. With it, while you type in a text field, it reads:

  • The text around your cursor: up to 4,000 characters before it, up to 200 after it, and any text you have selected.
  • Where you are typing: the app’s name and the window title, such as the subject of an email.
  • The chat above the box: in a chat, the newest 1,200 characters shown above the box you type in. It reads them again every second or two while you pause. This is on by default; you can switch it off.
  • Your name as set in macOS.
  • The keys you press, to know when you type and to take Tab, ⌘ → and Esc while a suggestion shows. Key presses are not saved.
  • Your notes: the Markdown files in ~/TypistLocal/knowledge/.
  • The clipboard: what you copied in the last 10 minutes, so a ticket number you copied can be suggested as you type it. Items that password managers mark as concealed are never read. You can turn this off in Settings. The notes panel (⌘ ⇧ Space) searches your notes with the clipboard text when the field has no text.

All of this goes to the model running in the app, on your Mac, and nowhere else.

What it never reads

Password fields. The app checks whether a field is a password field before it reads anything from it. If it is, the app stops there, so a password never enters the app, not even to be thrown away.

Login dialogs, browser address bars and search fields get the same treatment. Nothing from them is read.

What it keeps

Everything the app stores stays on this Mac, in the places below.

Your writing

So suggestions can use your names, project words and sign-offs.

What
A message when you send it or leave the field. Only the part you wrote during that visit, at least 4 words, mostly typed rather than pasted, up to 1,500 characters. At most 2,000 messages; the oldest go first.
Where
~/Library/Application Support/TypistLocal/Writing/writing.log
Protection
Each message is encrypted (AES-GCM). The key is in your login keychain as “TypistLocal writing key”. A backup or a copy of the file shows no text. Only your user account can open the file.
Never kept
Password fields, address bars, search fields, login dialogs, terminals and password managers. You can add more apps.
Turn off
Menu bar icon → Learn From My Writing.
Delete
Menu bar icon → Forget My Writing… deletes everything kept. The key stays in your keychain until you uninstall.

Your notes and their search index

What
The Markdown files you put in the notes folder, and an index of them for searching. On first launch the app adds one file of examples.
Where
~/TypistLocal/knowledge/ and ~/TypistLocal/typist.sqlite
Protection
Not encrypted. The index is a copy of your own notes, rebuilt from them each time the app starts.
Delete
Delete your files. Empty examples.md rather than deleting it: a deleted one comes back at the next start. The index is rebuilt from what is left.

The model

What
The language model file, 3.85 GB. It holds nothing about you.
Where
~/Library/Application Support/TypistLocal/Models/
Delete
Delete the folder. Suggestions stop until it is back.

Keystrokes saved

What
Two numbers for the menu: keystrokes saved today and in total. No text.
Where
macOS preferences for com.coffeetableconsultancy.TypistLocal

Settings

What
Your choices, such as whether learning is on.
Where
macOS preferences for com.coffeetableconsultancy.TypistLocal
Delete
defaults delete com.coffeetableconsultancy.TypistLocal

No log

The app writes no log. A diagnostic log is written only if someone starts the app with logging switched on by hand, through environment variables. That log contains what you type, so delete it after use.

Network connections

The app makes two network connections, and no others:

  1. Model download. The setup window downloads the model once: 3.85 GB from huggingface.co (mradermacher/gemma-4-E2B-i1-GGUF).
  2. Update checks. The app reads an update feed hosted on GitHub and, when there is a new version, downloads it from GitHub. It asks you before it checks on its own; Check for Updates… in the menu checks at once.

Neither one carries what you type, your writing or your notes. As with any download, the server sees your IP address and the file requested.

Suggestions themselves never use the network: the built-in engine runs entirely on your Mac.

Optional: the Ollama engine

If you switch to the Ollama engine, an advanced setting, the app sends its prompts to Ollama on your own Mac at 127.0.0.1. That traffic does not leave the Mac.

Accounts, analytics, telemetry

None. The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting of its own.

This website

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other servers: no fonts, scripts or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep its own server logs.

Questions

Ask in an issue on GitHub. To remove the app and everything it keeps, follow the uninstall steps.
